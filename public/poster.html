1. Problem Statement
Rural Connectivity Gap: Frequent network fluctuations cause frozen video and dropped audio calls.
High Data Usage: Standard apps (Zoom/Meet) consume 1.5-3.0 Mbps, burning expensive mobile data.
Lost Learning: Rural students miss critical explanations during abrupt meeting disconnections.
2. Proposed Solution
Dynamic Bandwidth Detection: Real-time monitoring of RTT, downlink speed, and packet drop.
3-Tier Adaptive Degradation: Seamless transition between Video → Audio-Only → Low-data Captions.
AI Summary & Transcript: Automated post-meeting minutes, action items, and downloadable summaries.
